# Admin core, slice 1: plan rates, master lists, settings

Port of Pallav's MealPlanRateController, MasterDataController and SettingsController. Code: `web-app/src/server/services/admin/`, repositories `repos/admin.ts` (+ the in-memory double `repos/memory/admin.ts`), routes under `/api/v1/admin/`. Tests: `tests/server/admin.test.ts` (20, through the real route files). Admin never touches a credential: where a later admin action needs one it calls the CredentialService port (`services/auth/credentialPort.ts`, implemented by Backend 1).

## 1. Permissions
Plan rates and master lists: the `masters` module (view to read, edit to change). The catalog offers nothing above `edit` for it, so deleting needs no higher level (Pallav: SuperAdmin only; HMS lets whoever holds the module do it). **Settings: the hotel's SuperAdmin only** ("Only the SuperAdmin can open Settings.", 403), as Pallav's `superadmin` middleware. Every read and write is the signed-in hotel's own data.

## 2. Plan rates
- Panel: the active plans of the `hotel_plan` list in its order; every rate newest date first, grouped by lower-cased plan; today (IST); what the hotel owes Pallav Food this month (sum of the month's bills' plan food).
- Save: the plan must be an active plan of the list ("The selected plan is invalid."); rate per person in paise, 0 to 100000 rupees; a valid date; a note of at most 255 characters. **One price per plan per date**: a second one for the same date replaces it. Message "CP plan rate saved." The log says "Added Plan rate: CP from 01-10-2026" with Plan, Rate per person, Applies from, Note, or "Changed Plan rate: ... (rate_per_person)" with the change.
- Delete: "Rate removed. Bills already made keep the amount they were saved with." A saved rate is what billing prices a stay from (the same rows).
- Deviation: the rate is whole paise (an integer), Pallav took a decimal number of rupees.

## 3. Master lists
- The rail: a plain list says "N in use"; a list that exists once per business (Revenue depositors, Expense receivers and the Miscellaneous catalogue) is **one entry** that opens on the hotel's first section.
- A list: its entries in order; a list of names (depositors, receivers) alphabetically with case ignored; an expense catalogue is flagged (`isCatalog`) and its categories and priced items are a separate screen.
- Scope for a grouped list: `both` (the default), `hotel` or `food`. "Both" reads the hotel's list and keeps only the entries both businesses have (shared); a business reads everything it has with shared ones marked. Adding on "both" adds the name to both lists once; renaming, hiding and removing keep the twin in step; narrowed to one business only that one changes.
- Rules and words: label required and at most 60 ("The label field is required."); the value defaults to the label; a value already on the list (case ignored) is refused ("The value has already been taken."); a list of names keeps only the name ("That name is already on this list."); a colour of at most 7 characters; only one default per list. A hidden entry leaves the forms but records that already use it keep it. Moving swaps places with the neighbour; at an end nothing happens.
- A list the app itself uses (`isSystem`) cannot be removed ("This list is used by the app itself and cannot be removed. You can still change what is in it.", 409).
- Every change writes a user-audit row (`master.item_created|updated|deleted`, `master.set_deleted`).

## 4. Settings
Groups (chat-app messaging and the encryption screens are left out, MJ): General, Bill header (hotel and food GSTIN, MJ #20 A), Email, Razorpay Hotel, Razorpay Food, Backup, Recycle Bin. A save reads every field of the group: an empty value falls back to its default, an unsent toggle reads as off, a select outside its options falls back to its default; an email and a number are checked in Pallav's words; a GSTIN is checked (15 characters) and kept upper case; a time of day is a 24-hour time. **A secret is stored as typed and never returned: the field answers `set: true|false` and `value: null`; an empty secret in a save keeps what is saved; the log says "changed", never the value.** The site name, address, support phone and both GSTINs are what a bill prints (the header reads the same settings). The log is module `settings`, "General settings saved", with each label and its new value.

## 5. Deviations (each with its test)
1. Plan-rate money is an integer of paise (test "saving").
2. The masters module has no separate delete level (test "permissions and isolation").
3. A GSTIN and a time of day are checked on save, which Pallav does not (tests "the bill header", "Pallav's checks and words").
4. A save with a field not sent reads it as empty/off exactly as Pallav; an unknown setting key is refused 422 (test "Pallav's checks and words").

## 6. Display preferences and the global search
**Preferences** (`GET`/`PATCH /api/v1/profile/preferences`): any signed-in person reads and changes their OWN day or night choice, `{uiTheme: "light" | "dark" | null}`; only the fields sent change; null clears the choice; anything else is 422 "Choose day or night."; no id of another person can be sent (strict body). Stored on the person (`platform.users.ui_theme`, a column Backend 1 adds; the in-memory double stands in until then). Test: `tests/server/admin.profile.test.ts`.

**Search** (`GET /api/v1/search?q=`): one question over the modules the person may READ, each source being the same repository search its own screen uses (every word must match, case-insensitive; plain text, so no blind index): bills (`bills.bills`), advances (`bills.advances`), companies and customers (`companies`), staff of every payroll company (`payroll.staff`), reservations (`pms.reservations`), stays (`pms.stays`), website enquiries (`website.enquiries`). **A source the person cannot view is never asked.** A question under 2 characters answers nothing; it is cut at 100 characters; at most 8 hits are asked of each source and 50 are returned; about 20 searches a minute per person (429 with `Retry-After`). Hit: `{kind, id, title, subtitle, companyId?}` (staff carry their payroll company); the answer also has `counts` per kind.

## 7. Users, roles and the signed-in person's own account

Source: Pallav `UserController`, `AccessController`, `ProfileController`, `UserHierarchy`. Code: `services/admin/{userService,roleService,accountService,userSchemas}.ts`, repositories `repos/adminUsers.ts` (interfaces) and `repos/memory/adminUsers.ts` (double over the same platform data as sign-in), tests `tests/server/admin.users.test.ts` (41).

**Credentials are never touched.** Admin creates, edits and closes the *account*; every credential effect goes through the `CredentialService` port: `issueInvite` (new account, status invited, unusable password placeholder, must change), `adminResetPassword`, `changeEmail` (users screen and own profile), `adminDisableTwoFactor`. A failed invite mail never undoes the account (Pallav); a refused e-mail change shows on the e-mail field in the port's sentence.

**Users** (`users` screen, MJ's four levels): list/get view; create = create; edit/toggle/unlock/reset password/disable emailed code = edit (reset also needs the `users.password` ability); delete = full. Rank (UserHierarchy::canManage): not yourself, never the SuperAdmin, only a strictly lower rung; a role offered at creation is a rung below the caller's own. Deleting the SuperAdmin is 409 "...Transfer the SuperAdmin role to an Admin first." Deactivating ends the person's sessions. Pallav's field words are used (username taken, e-mail needed because the invitation goes to it, mobile 10 digits stored `+91...`).
**Per-person exceptions** (`access` edit): give/take/clear one module level; you cannot give away what you do not hold; the access screen is never given to a person; SuperAdmin and your own account cannot be changed.
**Roles** (`access`): view reads, edit changes. A new role sits one rung under the caller's; ticks are checked like exceptions; the shipped four keep their names and cannot be deleted; a role people still hold is 409 "N accounts still use X. Move them first."; `access` above view is only ever on SuperAdmin; SuperAdmin cannot lose it. Every change is audited (`role.*`, `user.*`).
**Own account** (any signed-in person): profile (name, e-mail, phone required; username editable only by administrators and ignored otherwise), the last ten changes (sign-in rows excluded), devices (read only), sign-in history (own only; `ip` filter; every search word must match address, browser or a day written d-m-Y, d/m/Y or Y-m-d; 10/25/50/100 a page; totals and addresses by count) and removal of one *ended* sign-in (never the live one; audited as `signin.deleted`). Password change, emailed-code self switch and sessions are Backend 1's own routes (CredentialService), not repeated here. A place name needs a look-up service; `place` is "" until one exists.
**Engine:** PostgreSQL repositories for `AdminUsersUnit` do not exist yet (Backend 1); with a database configured these routes answer 503 rather than show an empty hotel.

**Known gaps (slice 4):**
1. **Place lookup.** Sign-in and device rows carry `place: ""`; an address-to-place look-up service (and its terms of use) is not chosen. The `places` summary is therefore by address. Search and the `ip` filter work on the address.
2. **SuperAdmin transfer.** Pallav's Profile > transfer (give the SuperAdmin role to an Admin) is not built: it changes two accounts' roles and ends sessions, so it needs a credential-side method on `CredentialService` (confirmation by emailed code) from Backend 1. Until then the SuperAdmin account cannot be deleted or changed (409/403 with Pallav's words).

## 8. Accounting years (Books)

Source: Pallav `BooksController`, `Support/Books.php` (years, cannotStart, next, startNext, cannotRemove, counts, removeLatest, carried). Code: `services/admin/booksService.ts`, repository interface `repos/adminBooks.ts`, double `repos/memory/adminBooks.ts`, tests `tests/server/admin.books.test.ts` (11). The accounting-year *lock* that every module applies on save is `services/cash/books.ts` (guardBooks), unchanged.

- **Panel** (`GET /admin/books`, Admins only): years newest first, each with what it holds (a count per dated table, Pallav's names), the next year's shape, `cannotStart` / `cannotRemove` (Pallav's sentences, or null) and what the current year carried in (cash in hand, debit bills owed, advances with money left, payroll advances unsettled, salaries unpaid; paise).
- **Start** (`POST /admin/books/start`): an Admin, only after the current year's last day ("2026-27 runs until 31 Mar 2027. The next year can be started from 01 Apr 2027." otherwise, 409). The current year is closed (closed_at/closed_by) and the next (1 April to 31 March) opened in ONE step; system-log line "Started accounting year ...".
- **Remove newest** (`DELETE /admin/books/latest {confirm}`): the SuperAdmin only (403 otherwise), never the only year (409), and the person types `DELETE <label>` exactly (422 on `confirm`). The engine gives back what the year did to older records, deletes everything dated in it and opens the previous year again, all or nothing; the message counts the entries removed. System-log line "Removed accounting year ... and everything dated in it".
- **Engine work (Backend 1):** `BooksRepo.counts`, `carried` and `removeLatest` reach every module's tables (Books::DATED, 15 tables) in one transaction. `removeLatest` order is Pallav's: (1) reverse each attendance month's salary run (gives back what it recovered); (2) older salaries paid this year back to Pending (paid amount 0, no date, no reference); (3) this year's bills give back what they took from older advances (advance balances + the bill's advance-delete amounts); (4) older debit bills settled this year back to waiting (delete their advance receipt, clear debit dates/modes/amounts, balance = total); (5) older advances refunded this year no longer refunded (balances back, refund fields cleared); (6) children before parents (expense lines, attendance entries, salary lines), then every dated row, bills before advances; (7) delete the year, reopen the previous. `carried` = Books::carried (cash = the daily sheet's opening on the year's first day, null when it cannot be worked out).
- **HMS deviations:** which years are on screen is the app's own choice, sent to the lists as `from`/`to`; the server keeps no per-person "viewing" state (Pallav kept it in the session). Money is paise.

## 9. Recycle bin

Source: Pallav `RecycleBinController`, `Support/RecycleBin.php`. Code: `services/admin/recycleService.ts` (the bin), `compose/recycle.ts` (adapters), tests `tests/server/admin.recycle.test.ts` (9, fake sources) and `admin.recycle.sources.test.ts` (5, the real bill and advance services).
- The hotel's SuperAdmin only (403 otherwise). Lists every kind newest deletion first with type, record, a line to tell two apart, who deleted it and **days left** (Settings `recycle.days`, 30 by default, never under 1; days elapsed are whole days). Search matches type, record, line and person; 10/25/50/100 a page.
- **Sources:** each kind is a `RecycleSource` {key, label, list, restore, purge, purgeBefore}. The module that owns the record lists its deleted rows and restores/removes with ITS OWN service, so the accounting-year lock, unique numbers, advance money and permission rule are the module's, and the module writes its own "recycle-bin" system-log line ("Restored by ..."); the bin adds none. A module's refusal stands and the record stays.
- **Routes:** GET `admin/recycle`, POST `admin/recycle/{key}/{id}/restore`, DELETE `admin/recycle/{key}/{id}`; item `{key, id, kind, label, detail, deletedBy, deletedAt, daysLeft}`; unknown kind or record not in the bin is 404.
- **Nightly clean-up:** `purgeExpired(hotelId)` removes, as the system and one transaction per record, what has sat longer than the keep time (a company still named on a bill stays, as when removed by hand). The job worker calls it per hotel.
- **Built so far:** bill, advance, company. **Still to add (same adapter pattern, one per module):** hotel/food deposit, withdrawal and expense, shift handover (cash); employee, bonus, payroll advance, separation, meal period (payroll, per company).
