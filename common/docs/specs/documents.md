# Printable documents as real PDFs (B7, MJ #22)

Engine: **pdfkit** behind the `DocumentRenderer` port (Backend Review/07-PDF-DECISION.md). Code: `web-app/src/server/documents/` (`kit.ts` layout kit, one file per kind, `renderer.ts` the port). Tests: `tests/server/documents.test.ts` and the per-module route tests.

## Rules
1. **Same data as the preview.** A document is drawn from the DTO its screen already gets (bill and advance: `BillPreview` / `AdvancePreview`; payroll: the service's `PayrollDoc` built from the slip / register / report / voucher DTO; statement: the statement rows). No second calculation.
2. **One renderer for structured documents.** `payrollDoc` draws a `PayrollDoc` (heading, para, label/value pairs, table with footer, total with words, signatures): salary slip, payment register, period and monthly reports, advance / bonus vouchers, separation record, joining and experience letters, staff record sheet, company statement. `cashReceipt` draws a `CashReceipt` (expense receipts, handover) for the cash module's routes.
3. **Fonts** bundled in `documents/fonts/` (SIL OFL): Inter for Latin, digits and the rupee sign; Noto Sans Gujarati and Devanagari for names in those scripts (runs switch font per script).
4. **Safety.** Control and direction-override characters dropped; one field prints at most 600 characters; at most 200 pages; a per-process queue (2 at a time, 50 waiting, then 429 with `retryAfterSeconds`); a 15 s deadline checked inside the drawing calls (drawing is synchronous); no network, no disk write; text is data (markup is printed, never interpreted).
5. **Endpoint.** `GET .../pdf` -> `application/pdf`, `Content-Disposition: inline; filename="..."`, `Cache-Control: no-store`, `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff`, trace id header. Same permission, same limits and same isolation as the preview; failures are the normal JSON error body.
6. **Bank and ID numbers on printed/shared copies (decided 11 Oct against the Pallav reference, `resources/views`; MJ can overrule):** staff record sheet `payroll/pdf/employee.blade.php` prints account and ID proof numbers **in full**; payroll company PDF `payroll/pdf/company.blade.php` prints the account number **in full**; customer profile PDF `customer/pdf.blade.php` prints the ID number **in full**; the salary slip `payroll/pdf/salary-slip.blade.php:239-240` prints the account number as bullets plus the **last four characters** (`slipAccount`). Pallav has no guest portal: the guest's own screen keeps the safe default, **masked** (`XXXX` plus last four, `maskId`), and sending it back unchanged keeps the stored number. Internal staff screens show full numbers.
7. **Industry format (5 Nov 2026).** White letterhead: the issuer on the left (name, address, phone, GSTIN; a logo when `Issuer.logo` carries one), the title block on the right (title, copy line, number, date); one thin gold rule (#C9A227) is the only colour, everything else graphite, so a black-and-white print loses nothing. Tables: light grey header between graphite rules, repeated on every page; figures right-aligned in tabular digits; a row never splits. Totals bold under a graphite rule, the amount in words under every money total. Footer on every page: "This is a computer-generated document." (not on signed letters), the title and the generated date and time ("05 Nov 2026, 3:05 pm"), "Page 1 of 3", the Easy Hotel Solutions mark.
   - **Tax invoice** (hotel with a GSTIN): "Original for recipient", invoice number and date, place of supply (the state of the hotel's GSTIN; a hotel stay is supplied where the hotel stands, so CGST + SGST), reverse charge "No", the guest's or company's GSTIN and state, per business: an HSN/SAC column on every charge line with the codes stored on the bill (996311 accommodation, 996331 food; an older bill read without them prints these), GST rate (shown only when the GST is exactly a slab of the taxable value: the bill does not store a rate), taxable value, CGST and SGST, V.P.O. outside GST, total in words; declaration and authorised signatory. Without a GSTIN the paper is a "Bill" with none of that.
   - **Letters** (file name `joining-letter-*` / `experience-letter-*`): business-letter layout with "Ref. JL|EL/<year>/<record>", date, addressee, title, subject, body at reading size, "Yours faithfully, For <company>", signatory; the acceptance block under a dashed line.
   - **Salary slip**: earnings and deductions side by side when both fit on the page.
8. **Mail attachments.** `pdfRenderer` (compose/payrollCore) renders the staff record sheet and the appointment letter with the SENDER's permissions and answers `ready` with the bytes; the outbox row carries only the kind and ids, and the worker renders again at send time (nothing stored). Until the outbox worker and SMTP exist the mail port is still the stub, so no mail leaves yet.

## Known limits
- Reports are A4 portrait (10 columns is tight); landscape for wide reports is a follow-up if the layout review asks for it.
- The hotel's own logo is supported by the kit but no service passes it yet (the bill, advance and payroll headers carry no logo bytes).
