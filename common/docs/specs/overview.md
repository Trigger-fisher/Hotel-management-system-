# Behaviour spec: Overview (Hotel dashboard, Daily Report, Food Plan Report)

Source: Pallav `app/Http/Controllers/{DashboardController,ReportController}.php`, `app/Services/Reports/{DailySheet,FoodPlanMonth}.php`, `app/Support/Books.php` (carried). Contract: `ui-shared/dashboards/overview.ts`. Code: `services/overview/{reportService,foodPlanMath,dashboardService,schemas}.ts`, repository interfaces `repos/reports.ts`, double `repos/memory/reports.ts`, PostgreSQL `repos/postgres/reports.ts` (Worker A), compose `compose/overview.ts`, tests `tests/server/overview.test.ts` (13). Money is paise; dates are the hotel's IST day.

## 1. The Daily Report (DailySheet)
Lines of one day are built by the repository (rules in `repos/reports.ts` SheetLine): bills per side (hotel, food) with a total above 0, amount = max(0, side total - advance taken), advances taken, advances refunded (out), debit recovery (five settlement slots), the six cash books (collection, withdrawal, expense) and cash staff advances. The SERVICE then:
- **Buckets** each line by its mode, trimmed and case-insensitive: `complimentary` -> free; `debit` -> debit; `cash` -> cash; empty -> banked when the amount is above 0, cash when it is 0 (a line that came to nothing is still shown, with the cash); anything else (UPI, Card, OTA, Bank transfer...) -> banked. A line with no mode shows "Not set".
- **Columns:** cash in, cash out, banked (both directions), debit, complimentary; each ordered by room then reference (natural order, "9" before "10").
- **Tonight's plan meals** (FoodPlan for that one night) are paid to Pallav Food out of the drawer every day and are taken off.
- **Totals:** `cashOnHand = opening + cashIn`; `carryForward = opening + cashIn - cashOut - foodPlan`; `revenue = cashIn + bankedIn + debit`; `paidOut = cashOut + bankedOut + foodPlan`; `net = revenue - paidOut`; `cashPaidTotal = cashOut + foodPlan`; `entries` = lines of the day.

### The OPENING balance (the rule Worker A needed)
**Opening on day D = (sum of every cash line dated before D, in minus out) - (every plan meal of every night before D), counted from the beginning of time.** A line is a cash line when its mode is `cash` (trimmed, any case); UPI, card, debit and complimentary lines never move the drawer. Nothing resets it: not the accounting year (Books does not zero the drawer), not the shift handover (the handover's counted cash is a record, never an opening), not a month. The plan meals before D are the nights from 2000-01-01 to D-1 priced from the plan-rate history, exactly the Food Plan arithmetic below. D's closing (`carryForward`) is therefore D+1's opening.
**`carried.cashPaise` of an accounting year** (Books::carried) = the Daily Report opening on that year's first day (1 April). The books service asks the reports service (`openingCash`); when it cannot be worked out the figure is null (Pallav's try/catch), so the repository returns null for it.
PostgreSQL pushes the cash part down as `DaySheetRepo.cashNetBefore(day)`; the plan-meal part is computed in the service from `FoodPlanRepo.stayBills`.

## 2. The Food Plan Report (FoodPlanMonth)
For a calendar month (default this month), optionally one plan: each bill's stay is read night by night; only nights inside the window count, so a stay across two months is split between their reports; each night is priced at the plan rate in force THAT night (`MealPlanRate::rateOn`: the latest rate of the plan that had started by that night, 0 when none) times the persons of that part. Plan rates are the plain meal price: no GST, nothing from the guest's own bill. A bill with no saved stay parts uses arrival date, departure date, hotel plan and persons (1 when empty). A guest whose month comes to 0 is not a row. Rows sort by first night then room. `parts` run consecutive nights of the same plan, persons and rate together; `byPlan` sums nights by plan (person-nights, the distinct rates, amount); `plans` lists plans that carry a rate; `outside` is the nights of the stay in another month. The repository gives bills dated within the window plus or minus two months (Pallav's loose window); the exact nights are checked one by one.

## 3. The dashboard (DashboardController) and arrivals
`GET /api/v1/overview/dashboard`: today (IST), this month and last month up to the same day, the two cash books (deposits in; withdrawals, expenses and cash staff advances out), the 30-day trend, spending by head (six rows; the rest folded into "N other heads"), the 7 newest entries, the handover figures, payroll figures (`null` for a Hotel User, account role `hotel_user`, even one holding the payroll grant: the figures are not read and no salary item is added, MJ 5 Nov 2026; the same holds for `GET /overview/attention`), and what needs attention (no handover today; open salaries; nothing entered today), plus HMS's `arrivalsToday`: confirmed reservations with today as the check-in date (`ReservationRepo.arrivalsToday`; a checked-in, cancelled or no-show reservation does not count). Guest billing is not in these figures, as in Pallav.
**Recorder names:** the repository gives the user id and the name written on the row; the service shows the user's display name from the people directory and falls back to the row's name.

## 4. Routes and permissions
`GET /api/v1/reports/daily?date=` and `/reports/food-plan?month=&plan=` need `reports` at view; `/overview/dashboard` needs `dashboard` at view. A bad date or month is 422; unknown query keys are refused.
