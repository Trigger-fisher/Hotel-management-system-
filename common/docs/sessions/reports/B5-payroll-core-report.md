## Golden tests (real PHP)

- All 41 phone countries, and 320 numbers through split, normalise, check and format.
- The attendance save's cell rule:
  - unknown or inactive keys;
  - days outside the month;
  - clearing a day versus writing it;
  - overtime read as `(float)`, as PHP does.
- The progress percentage, using PHP's rounding.
- `isComplete` and `daysExpected` over month ends and leap years.
- Payable days and percentages are computed in `SalaryProcessor::processEmployee`, so their golden tests belong to B6.

## Scale

- The attendance grid is read in one query per page. A test counts the reads.
- The incomplete-staff check and the filled-day count are aggregates.
- A save is one batched write.
- Lists carry no sealed column.
- A page's deductions come from one read.

## Security

- **Sealed numbers**:
  - write-only (absent = keep, "" = remove);
  - reads show the last 4 digits;
  - the IFSC and the emergency number come back in full only on the single-record GET;
  - tests prove no plain number is stored.
- **Identity** comes only from the session:
  - `isAdmin` from the session and the permission catalog;
  - `sentBy` from the signed-in person;
  - the unlock is tied to `session.sessionId`.
- A test shows that another session's view of an unlocked month puts the lock back.
- The strict schemas reject `isAdmin` and `sentBy` in a request.

## Deviations (each in the spec)

1. Generate and regenerate answer 422 `VALIDATION_FAILED` with `details.fields.reason` = "Salary generation arrives with Payroll Pay." until B6 (Planning: NETWORK_OFFLINE would show "You're offline").
2. The accounting-year guard applies only where the month row is saved (generate, regenerate, unlock). This matches Pallav, whose trait is on `AttendanceMonth`; days are not guarded.
3. Saving a month that was never opened, or a future month, returns 404.
4. The rows in a save must be the company's live staff. Pallav accepted any posted id.
